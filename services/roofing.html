Valley Weather

What the Valley Does to a Roof

Roofs age faster down here. The sun runs hot most of the year and bakes the oils out of asphalt shingles until they go brittle and start losing their granules. You can see it in the gutters — that black grit is your roof wearing out.

Then spring brings hail, and wind coming off the Gulf gets under any shingle whose seal has already let go. A roof that would have lasted twenty years somewhere cooler often doesn't make it that far in Hidalgo County.

None of that means your roof is finished. It does mean a roof here earns a look every year or two once it hits fifteen — before a small opening turns into a soaked ceiling.

Which One Do I Need?

Fix It, or Replace It?

This is the question almost everyone calls with, and the honest answer is that nobody can give it to you from the driveway. It depends on how old the roof is, how much of it is worn, and what the decking underneath looks like.

Plenty of roofs we look at need a roof repair and nothing more — a few shingles, some flashing around a vent, a reseal where the last guy cut a corner. Others are worn through top to bottom, and patching them just moves the leak somewhere else. That's when a roof replacement is the cheaper answer over any real span of time.

We'll show you the photos from your own roof and tell you which one you're looking at. If it can be repaired, we'll say so — we're not in the business of selling roofs people don't need.

Materials

Shingles, Metal, and Impact-Resistant

Most homes in Pharr and McAllen wear architectural asphalt shingles, and for good reason — they look right on a Valley house, they come in every color, and they're the most common roof we install.

Metal roofing costs more up front and lasts a good deal longer. It sheds heat instead of soaking it up, which is worth something in August. Impact-resistant shingles are the third option — built with a tougher mat so hail is less likely to bruise them.

We'll lay out the choices during the inspection, with the trade-offs in plain language, and tell you what makes sense for your house and your street. No pressure to take the expensive one.

Good to Know

Roofing Questions We Get a Lot

Do you charge for a roof inspection?
No. Roof inspections are free anywhere we work in the Valley. We get on the roof, take photos of what we find, and walk you through it in plain language. If your roof is fine, we'll tell you that too.
How much does a new roof cost in Pharr?
It depends on the size of the roof, the pitch, the material you pick, and what the decking looks like once we pull the old shingles off. That's why we don't put a number on this page. We look at your roof for free and hand you a written quote before anyone starts work.
How long does a roof last in the Rio Grande Valley?
Less than the number on the wrapper, usually. Between the heat, the sun, and the hail that comes through in spring, asphalt shingle roofs down here tend to age faster than the same roof would up north. Once a roof is fifteen years old it's worth having someone look at it every year or two.
What kind of roofing do you install?
Mostly architectural asphalt shingles, which is what sits on most homes in Pharr and McAllen. We also install metal roofing and impact-resistant shingles built to handle hail better. We'll show you the options during the inspection and tell you what actually makes sense for your house.
Do you help with insurance claims?
We do. After a storm we document the damage with photos and measurements, and we'll help you file the claim and put together what your carrier asks for. What your policy covers is between you and your insurance company, but you won't be sorting out the paperwork by yourself. More on that on our storm restoration page.
How soon can someone come look at my roof?
Usually same day. Someone answers the phone 24 hours a day, seven days a week. If water is coming in right now, say so when you call — we do same-day emergency tarping to keep it out of your ceiling until we can do the real repair.
What makes you different from the crews that show up after a hail storm?
We're not going anywhere. Our office is on North Sugar Road in Pharr, the Better Business Bureau has had us accredited with an A+ rating since 2016, and Richard has spent 20+ years in the roofing trade. If something needs attention in two years, you'll still know where to find us.
Do you do work besides roofing?
Yes. Alongside roofing we handle home remodeling — kitchens, baths, flooring, drywall, windows and doors — and room additions when you need more space. Same crew, same standard.
