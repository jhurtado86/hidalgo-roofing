Good to Know

Common Questions

What areas do you serve?
We're based in Pharr and work across Hidalgo County and the Rio Grande Valley — McAllen, Edinburg, Mission, Weslaco, and San Juan, plus Alamo, Palmview, Donna, and Mercedes. If you're not sure whether we reach your street, just call and ask.
What happens when you come out to look at my roof?
We come out, get on the roof, take photos, and show you what we found. If the roof is fine, we'll tell you that too — and you'll get a clear quote before any work starts.
My roof is leaking right now. Can you come today?
Usually, yes. We answer the phone 24/7 and do same-day emergency roof tarping across the Valley. Getting a tarp on fast keeps water out of your ceiling and drywall while we sort out the real repair.
Do you help with insurance claims?
We do. After a storm we document the damage with photos and measurements, and we'll help you file the claim and pull together what your carrier asks for. What your policy covers is between you and your insurance company, but you won't be figuring out the paperwork alone.
Can you repair my roof, or do I need a whole new one?
Plenty of roofs we look at just need a repair — a few shingles, some flashing, a resealed vent. Others are worn through and need a replacement. Once we get up on the roof, we'll show you the photos so you can see which one you're looking at.
Do you do work besides roofing?
Yes. Alongside roofing we handle home remodeling — kitchens, baths, flooring, drywall, windows, and doors — and room additions when you need more space. Same crew, same standard.
