The Roofer Working Mission and Sharyland

Mission is the west end of the run we make every week. From our office on North Sugar Road in Pharr it is I-2 west through McAllen and out to the Shary Road and Conway exits. Citrus built this town — John Shary bought the land and planted the groves here a little over a hundred years ago — and plenty of Mission neighborhoods still have the big trees to show for it.

Those trees are part of why we get called. Limbs drop on shingles, leaves and citrus debris pack into valleys, and heavy shade keeps a slope damp long after the rest of the roof dries. We handle roof repair, full roof replacement, storm restoration, and remodeling inside the house — free inspection first.

Where We Work in Mission

Good to Know

Mission Questions

Do you cover Sharyland, or only the city of Mission?
Both. We work the Shary Road corridor, the Pioneer side, and the older Sharyland streets the same as anywhere else in town. If the address says Mission, TX, we come out.
Trees hang over my roof in Mission. Is that actually a problem?
It can be. Limbs scrape granules off shingles, leaves and citrus debris pack into valleys and hold water there, and heavy shade keeps a roof from drying out between rains. We look hard at the valleys and the shaded slopes on Mission houses, because that is where the damage usually starts.
How fast can you get to Mission after a hail storm?
Usually the same day. We are in Pharr, so Mission is a straight run west on I-2. If water is coming into the house, say so on the call — same-day emergency tarping happens before anything else gets scheduled.
Do you charge for a roof inspection in Mission?
No. Inspections are free everywhere we work. We get up on the roof, photograph what we find, and go through the photos with you. If the roof has years left in it, that is what you will hear.
Can you build a room addition in Mission, not just roof work?
Yes. Room additions are regular work for us — framing, wiring, plumbing and finish work — along with home remodeling across Mission. The roofing side just means the new roof ties into the old one properly.
